# Notice

Daily Scrapbook is an independently authored photo-journal workflow maintained by [zhaopu](https://github.com/zhaopu).

## Skill structure reference

The repository organization and documentation approach were informed by [Ian Xiaohei Illustrations](https://github.com/helloianneo/ian-xiaohei-illustrations), created by [Ian](https://github.com/helloianneo), including the separation of style DNA, composition guidance, prompt templates and quality checks.

The skill text and prompts in this repository were written for photo scrapbooks. No Xiaohei character, example illustration, source-code block or documentation passage from that project is bundled here. The reference is credited for its workflow structure.

## Visual research

Public photo-journal works by [墨奶茶 / guanerlili](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/user/profile/5fe41f980000000001000864) informed the study of photo hierarchy, cutouts, annotation placement and paper collage. This is not an official or endorsed project from that creator.

The published package does not contain that creator's photographs, family images or artwork, or the private photographs and generated family-journal pages used during local testing. Source links are attribution and optional further reading, not assets that the skill downloads by default.

## Included previews

The images in `assets/layouts/` and `daily-scrapbook/references/layout-overview.png` are original abstract wireframes. Their light-gray photo blocks and silhouettes are placeholders; they are not generated scrapbook results and do not contain real people or user photographs.
